* **ROIC** = $1,294.43 / $1,293.45 = \mathbf{100.08\%}$

### WACC 計算

* **資本結構權重**：Equity Weight ~98.3%, Debt Weight ~1.7%

$$\text{EVA} = (\text{ROIC} - \text{WACC}) \times \text{Invested Capital}$$
$$\text{EVA} = (100.08\% - 9.96\%) \times \$1,293.45 \text{ 億} = \mathbf{+\$1,165.65 \text{ 億}}$$
