### WACC 計算

- **權益成本 ($R_e$)** = $R_f (4.20\%) + \beta (1.069) \times ERP (5.50\%) = \mathbf{10.08\%}$
- **稅後債務成本 ($R_d$)** = $4.00\% \times (1 - 0.15) = \mathbf{3.40\%}$

- **WACC** = $98.3\% \times 10.08\% + 1.7\% \times 3.40\% = \mathbf{9.97\%} \approx \mathbf{9.8\%}$

$$\text{EVA} = (\text{ROIC} - \text{WACC}) \times \text{Invested Capital}$$
