### 3. 經濟附加價值（EVA）

$$\text{EVA} = (\text{ROIC} - \text{WACC}) \times \text{Invested Capital} = (88.60\% - 9.53\%) \times \$149,857\text{M} = +\$118,500\text{M}$$
