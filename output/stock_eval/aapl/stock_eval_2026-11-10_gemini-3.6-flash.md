## 7. ROIC / WACC 與經濟附加價值（EVA）分析

### ROIC 計算
- **NOPAT** = $\text{EBIT } (\$158,063M) \times (1 - 15\% \text{ 有效稅率}) = \$134,353.55M$

- **ROIC** = $\$134,353.55M / \$149,845M = \mathbf{89.66\%}$

### WACC 計算
- **權益成本 ($R_e$)** = $R_f (4.2\%) + \beta (1.089) \times \text{ERP} (5.5\%) = 10.19\%$
- **稅後債務成本 ($R_d$)** = $3.8\% \times (1 - 15\%) = 3.23\%$
- **資本結構權重**：權益權重 $E/V \approx 98.2\%$，債務權重 $D/V \approx 1.8\%$
- **WACC** = $(98.2\% \times 10.19\%) + (1.8\% \times 3.23\%) = \mathbf{10.07\%}$

### EVA 價值創造
- **ROIC vs WACC 利差** = $89.66\% - 10.07\% = \mathbf{+79.59\%}$
- **EVA（經濟附加價值）** = $79.59\% \times \$149,845M \approx \mathbf{\$119,261M}$
