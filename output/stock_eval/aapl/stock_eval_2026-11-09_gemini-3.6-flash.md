## 9. ROIC / WACC 與經濟附加價值（EVA）分析

### ROIC 計算

* **NOPAT** = EBIT × (1 − Tax Rate) = $158,063M × (1 − 16.38%) = $132,175M
* **Invested Capital** = 總權益 ($92,900M 估) + 總債務 ($94,479M) − 現金 ($56,722M) = **$130,657M**
* **ROIC** = $132,175M / $130,657M = **101.15%**

### WACC 計算

* **權益成本（Cost of Equity, Re）** = $Rf (4.25\%) + \beta (1.089) \times ERP (5.5\%) = \mathbf{10.24\%}$
* **稅後債務成本（After-tax Rd）** = $4.00\% \times (1 - 0.1638) = \mathbf{3.34\%}$
* **資本結構權重**：權益權重 (E/V) = 98.18%，債務權重 (D/V) = 1.82%
* **WACC** = $(98.18\% \times 10.24\%) + (1.82\% \times 3.34\%) = \mathbf{10.11\%}$

### EVA 經濟附加價值

$$\text{EVA} = (\text{ROIC} - \text{WACC}) \times \text{Invested Capital} = (101.15\% - 10.11\%) \times \$130,657\text{M} = \mathbf{+\$119,000\text{M}}$$

*結論：ROIC 遠大於 WACC（超額回報利差高達 91.04%），顯示公司每投資 $1 美元皆在創造巨大的股東經濟價值。*
